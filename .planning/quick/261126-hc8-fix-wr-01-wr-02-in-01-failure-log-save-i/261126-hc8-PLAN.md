Fix code-review findings WR-01, WR-02 and IN-01 (see .planning/phases/02-panel-za-traefik-s-zaschischennym-vhodom/02-REVIEW.md) in the stack-start rescue of roles/panel/tasks/stack.yml.

Root cause: the mkdir and copy tasks use `failed_when: false`, which overwrites `failed` to false, so the `is succeeded` guards are always true. The copy runs even when the mkdir failed (WR-01) and the fail message claims the logs were saved even when the copy failed (WR-02).

LOCKED user decision (D-01): replace `failed_when: false` on the mkdir and the copy with a nested block/rescue that sets fact `xvpn_panel_start_log_saved: true` only after both succeed, and `false` in the nested rescue. The final fail message conditions on `xvpn_panel_start_log_saved | default(false)`. `ignore_errors` is forbidden (explicitly rejected by the user). A `stat`-based existence check is forbidden (a stale log from an earlier failed run would give a false positive).

D-02: move the hardcoded /var/log/xvpn path (mkdir path, copy dest, fail message, post-block cleanup) into the role variable `xvpn_panel_failure_log_dir`, default `/var/log/xvpn`, in defaults/main.yml and meta/argument_specs.yml, and document it in README.md. Keep `no_log: true` and `diff: false` on the copy.

Output: modified stack.yml, defaults, argument_specs, README.
</objective>
