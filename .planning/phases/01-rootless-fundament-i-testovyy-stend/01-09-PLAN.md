## Phase Goal

**As an** operator, **I want to** run one playbook on a clean Rocky/Alma 10 host, **so that** I get a verified rootless host where a dedicated user with linger runs a Quadlet service on port 80 that survives reboot under SELinux enforcing with zero AVCs, all checked automatically by Molecule and CI linters.

This plan's slice: **the VM scenario that proves on real enforcing EL10 VMs that the rootless :80 service survives a reboot with zero AVCs; Plan 10 runs it in CI.**

<objective>
Build the Molecule VM scenario (QA-03) on AlmaLinux 10 and Rocky 10 with SELinux enforcing: converge, idempotence, reboot, runtime + label + zero-AVC assertions.
